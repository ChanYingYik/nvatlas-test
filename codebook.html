Coding scheme · version 2

Four ways to name what a nominal does.

The categories describe the core meaning of the English nominal in its sentence pair. They do not describe the quality of the translation.

01 · Communication

Speech & thought

An utterance, communicative act, representation, or act of thinking is central to the English nominal. Typical terms include question, explanation, discussion and description.

Theory source
Halliday and Martin's grammatical metaphor: a process may be packaged as an entity.
Decision rule
Choose this category when the nominal names saying, asking, telling, representing or thinking. Ignore the Chinese verb's grammar when deciding the semantic category.
Positive example
ID 168: question → 问 in "I was gonna ask you the same question." The nominal names a communicative act.
Negative example
ID 474: obsession → 痴迷 is an affective state, not an utterance or explicit thought act.
Boundary case
Argument can mean a disagreement or a reason. The supplied entries describe disputes, so they remain under communication; a future logical-argument sense would need review.

01 · 言語

言語與思考

英文名詞的核心是話語、溝通行為、再現方式或思考行為。典型詞包括 question、explanation、discussion 與 description。

理論來源
Halliday 與 Martin 的語法隱喻：過程可以被包裝為實體。
判定規則
名詞命名說、問、告知、再現或思考時歸入此類。判斷時看名詞核心意義，不以中文動詞的語法外形決定。
正向例子
編號 168：question → 问，對應 "I was gonna ask you the same question."；名詞指向溝通行為。
反向例子
編號 474：obsession → 痴迷 是情感狀態，不是話語或明確思考行為。
邊界案例
Argument 可指爭論，也可指理由。提供資料中的例子描述爭執，因此暫歸言語；若日後出現邏輯論證義，須重新審核。

02 · Control

Law & control

The nominal concerns permission, obligation, institutional judgement, legal process, punishment, management or a binding social outcome. Examples include permission, decision, offence and annulment.

Theory source
Vinay and Darbelnet's transposition, used with a semantic domain distinction rather than as a strict equivalence rule.
Decision rule
Choose this category when the core meaning depends on authority, rules, social consequence or a decision that constrains action.
Positive example
ID 5884: permission → 准许 in "With your permission... I'll pull a few eggs from under your nose." The nominal concerns authorisation.
Negative example
ID 2036: dance → 跳舞 is performed action rather than legal authority or permission.
Boundary case
Consideration can mean thought, care or a legal exchange. Supplied uses occur in legal or transactional phrasing, so it is coded as law and control, not communication.

02 · 控制

法律與控制

名詞涉及許可、義務、制度判斷、法律程序、懲罰、管理或具約束力的社會結果。 例子包括 permission、decision、offence 與 annulment。

理論來源
Vinay 與 Darbelnet 的詞性轉換，配合語意領域區分，而非把它當成固定等值規則。
判定規則
名詞核心意義涉及權力、規則、社會後果，或限制行動的決定時歸入此類。
正向例子
編號 5884：permission → 准许，句中意義是「取得准許」，與授權和行動條件有關。
反向例子
編號 2036：dance → 跳舞 是實際演出動作，不是法律權限或准許。
邊界案例
Consideration 可指思考、體諒或法律上的對價。提供資料的用法位於法律及交易語境，因此歸入法律與控制，而非言語與思考。

03 · Affect

Emotion & state

The nominal foregrounds a feeling, attention, inner state, desire, perception or evaluation. Examples include obsession, attention, silence and intoxication.

Theory source
Grammatical metaphor and systemic-functional attention to affect and state as meanings that can be packaged nominally.
Decision rule
Choose this category when the nominal primarily names what a person feels, attends to, desires or experiences internally, rather than an outward event.
Positive example
ID 474: obsession → 痴迷 in "What is his obsession with you?" describes a strong affective fixation.
Negative example
ID 804: destruction → 烧毁 is an outward destructive event, not an inner state.
Boundary case
Attention can mark a cognitive action, but the supplied lines mostly express focus or a command to attend. It is coded as a state of attention so that the common subtitle pattern remains visible.

03 · 情感

情緒與狀態

名詞把感受、注意、內在狀態、欲望、感知或評價放在前景。例子包括 obsession、attention、silence 與 intoxication。

理論來源
語法隱喻，以及系統功能語言學對情感與狀態可被名詞化包裝的觀察。
判定規則
名詞主要命名個人的感受、注意、欲望或內在經驗，而非外在事件時歸入此類。
正向例子
編號 474：obsession → 痴迷，描述強烈的情感執著。
反向例子
編號 804：destruction → 烧毁 是外在破壞事件，不是內在狀態。
邊界案例
Attention 也可視為認知行動；提供資料多表示專注或「注意」的指令，因此編為注意狀態，使字幕中的高頻模式保持可見。

04 · Process

Action & change

The nominal names an outward activity, event, transformation, construction, movement or physical process. Examples include destruction, publication and dance.

Theory source
Grammatical metaphor, where a process is compressed into a noun and may be unpacked again in the target language.
Decision rule
Choose this category when no other domain is central and the nominal primarily names something happening, being done or changing.
Positive example
ID 804: destruction → 烧毁 names a physical destructive action.
Negative example
ID 5884: permission → 准许 concerns authorisation, even though it is also an act.
Boundary case
Administration can be an activity, but in institutional usage it is treated as management and therefore coded as law and control.

04 · 過程

行動與變化

名詞命名外在活動、事件、轉變、建造、移動或物理過程。例子包括 destruction、publication 與 dance。

理論來源
語法隱喻：過程先被壓縮成名詞，再可能於目標語中展開為動作。
判定規則
若名詞不屬其他領域，且主要命名正在發生、被完成或正在改變的事情，歸入此類。
正向例子
編號 804：destruction → 烧毁 命名具體的破壞行動。
反向例子
編號 5884：permission → 准许 雖也是行為，核心意義卻是授權。
邊界案例
Administration 可以是活動，但制度語境中主要指管理，因此歸入法律與控制。

Supporting field

Verb position

This field is mechanical and separate from semantic coding. After removing surrounding punctuation, the script locates the supplied Chinese verb.

  • Verb-first: the verb is the first meaningful element.
  • Modal or negative context: the four characters before the verb contain a marker such as 不, 沒, 別, 要, 會, 能 or 請.
  • Embedded in predicate: the verb occurs later without one of those markers immediately before it.

This is a rule-based approximation. It does not parse syntax and should not be read as a complete grammatical analysis.

輔助欄位

動詞位置

這個欄位機械化產生，與語意編碼分開。腳本先移除譯文兩端的標點， 再找出提供的中文動詞。

  • 動詞起首： 動詞是有意義的第一個成分。
  • 情態或否定語境： 動詞前四個字內含有不、沒、別、要、會、能、請等標記。
  • 嵌入謂語： 動詞出現在較後位置，前方沒有上述標記。

這是規則式近似，並非完整句法分析，讀者不應把它當作嚴格語法標註。
