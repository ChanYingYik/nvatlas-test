AI use declaration

Tools used: OpenAI Codex in the Codex desktop application, backed by a GPT-5 model. The exact checkpoint was not disclosed by the environment. Web research was limited to the supplied brief, the supplied CSV and the public course example repository.

I asked AI to read the project brief, inspect the CSV, design the corpus schema and site, write the HTML/CSS/JavaScript, generate reproducible data metadata, draft the bilingual codebook/report and test the interactions. AI also drafted the initial four-way semantic coding.

The student supplied the corpus and requirements. The student must still confirm the original corpus source, personally verify 50 entries, review the AI-assisted coding, and decide whether the analysis and bilingual report accurately represent their work before submission.

AI 使用聲明

使用工具：Codex 桌面應用程式中的 OpenAI Codex，背後使用 GPT-5 模型； 執行環境未披露確切模型檢查點。網路研究只用於核對提供的作業要求、提供之 CSV， 以及公開的課堂範例網站。

AI 協助閱讀專題要求、檢查 CSV、設計語料欄位與網站、撰寫 HTML/CSS/JavaScript、產生可重現的資料中介資訊、草擬雙語編碼簿與報告，並測試互動功能。 初步四類語意編碼也由 AI 草擬。

語料與作業要求由學生提供。提交前，學生仍須確認原始語料來源、親自核對五十筆資料、 檢視 AI 協助的分類，並判斷分析與雙語報告是否準確代表自己的成果。

Division of work

Human and AI contributions

Student

Supplied the project brief and 500-row CSV; selects the final topic, verifies entries against the corpus, reviews categories, and owns the submitted interpretation.

AI

Inspected requirements, transformed the CSV, coded the interface, drafted bilingual text, and generated reproducible analysis fields.

Not completed by AI: no row is falsely marked as human checked. The quality-check page records the student's own review locally and exports the result for a later data update.

工作分工

人與 AI 的貢獻

學生

提供專題要求及五百行 CSV；選定最後題目，對照原始語料核對資料、審核類別，並對提交的最終解釋負責。

AI

檢查要求、轉換 CSV、撰寫介面、草擬雙語文字，並產生可重現的分析欄位。

AI 未代替完成： 網站沒有把任何資料假稱為人工核對。 品質檢查頁只在使用者自己的瀏覽器中記錄審核結果，並可匯出供日後更新資料。

Prompt examples

Two prompts that worked well

1. Start from the brief

"Read the attached project brief and implement it using the supplied NV Atlas CSV. Do not invent corpus entries."

2. Specify the interaction contract

"Build a static bilingual explorer with network, treemap and heatmap views. Make every view respond to search and filters, link clicks to the table, and store the exact state in the URL."

One thing AI got wrong

AI initially treated the CSV as a publication-ready corpus. Inspection showed that it contained only five columns and no source, category or human-check status. The build therefore generated analysis metadata, exposed the provenance uncertainty, and left human verification at no instead of filling in convenient but unsupported claims.

提示詞示例

兩個有效的提示詞

一、從要求開始

「閱讀附件的專題要求，並用提供的 NV Atlas CSV 實作。不要虛構語料。」

二、明確規定互動

「建立靜態雙語探索器，包含網絡、樹狀圖與熱圖。所有檢視須回應搜尋及篩選，點擊要連動資料表，並把完整狀態保存在網址。」

AI 曾做錯的一件事

AI 起初把 CSV 視為可直接公開的完整語料。檢查後發現它只有五個欄位， 沒有來源、類別或人工核對狀態。因此建置流程產生分析中介資訊， 清楚揭露來源不確定性，並把人工核對保持為 no， 而沒有填入方便但缺乏證據的完成聲明。

Source and limitations

Data notes

Supplied file

NVAtlas-AI-Agent.csv, 500 rows, modified 2026-10-30.

Reported corpus

OpenSubtitles 2024 en-zh_CN. This label matches the course example and sentence IDs, but needs confirmation against the original download.

Preserved fields

English, Chinese, English nominal and Chinese verb. Eight repeated sentence pairs remain because they have different source IDs.

Unverified fields

The sampling seed and collection date are not present in the supplied CSV. The OpenSubtitles link and the 2026-10-30 file date are metadata, not proof of the missing record.

Corpus source link: OpenSubtitles in OPUS. Sentence pairs in this project are short subtitle lines, not complete episodes or scripts.

Reproduce the browser data with python3 scripts/build_corpus.py. The script preserves source fields, checks for 500 non-empty rows, applies the published category map, calculates verb position and writes data/corpus.json.

來源與限制

資料說明

提供檔案

NVAtlas-AI-Agent.csv，五百行，修改時間為 2026-10-30。

標示語料

OpenSubtitles 2024 en-zh_CN。此標示符合課堂範例與句編號格式，但仍須對照原始下載確認。

保留欄位

英文、中文、英文名詞與中文動詞。八組重複句對因原始句編號不同而保留。

未核實欄位

提供 CSV 沒有抽樣種子與收集日期。OpenSubtitles 連結及 2026-10-30 檔案日期只是中介資訊，不能當作缺失紀錄的證明。

語料來源連結： OPUS 中的 OpenSubtitles。 本站只使用短字幕句對，不使用整部劇集或完整劇本。

可用 python3 scripts/build_corpus.py 重現瀏覽器資料。 腳本會保留原文欄位、檢查五百行皆非空、套用公開類別對照、計算動詞位置， 並輸出 data/corpus.json。

Public site

Copyright, privacy and sensitive language

The site contains short excerpts from subtitle sentence pairs for coursework and corpus analysis. It does not reproduce whole works. Source and sentence IDs are retained. Before final publication, confirm that the original corpus licence permits this use and link to its terms.

The supplied rows appear to be film dialogue rather than personal data. No phone number, address or private account information is deliberately added. Some subtitle dialogue contains profanity or mature themes; the explorer carries a visible content warning.

公開網站

版權、隱私與敏感語言

網站為課堂及語料分析使用而展示短字幕句對，不重製完整作品，並保留來源與句編號。 最終公開前，應確認原始語料授權允許此用途，並連結相關條款。

提供資料看來是影視對話，而非個人資料。本專案沒有刻意加入電話、地址或私人帳戶資料。 部分字幕含有粗俗語言或成人主題，因此探索器設有明顯內容提示。
