Learning report

Nouns under pressure, verbs at work.

A small bilingual corpus study of how English nominal meanings are reorganised as Chinese verbs in subtitles.

學習報告

名詞承受壓力，動詞開始工作。

一項小型英中雙語語料研究，觀察字幕翻譯如何把英文名詞的意義重新組織為中文動詞。

01 · Origin

Why this topic?

I began with a simple contrast that is easy to miss in a subtitle: English often packages an action or a feeling as a noun, while the Chinese line turns that meaning into a verb. The supplied data already marked an English nominal and a Chinese verb for each sentence pair, so the topic emerged from a visible pattern rather than from a list of invented examples.

I chose the title Nominal / Verb because the slash captures the project's central movement. The first side names what English foregrounds; the second side names what the Chinese translation often asks the reader to process as an action or state.

01 · 緣起

為何選擇這個題目？

我從字幕中一個容易忽略的對照出發：英文常把動作或感受包裝成名詞， 中文卻可能把同一意義展開為動詞。使用者提供的資料已為每個句對標出 英文名詞與中文動詞，因此題目源自可見的語言模式，而非憑空設計的例子。

專題名稱 Nominal / Verb 以斜線象徵核心移動：英文把什麼放在前景， 中文又如何要求讀者把它理解成動作或狀態。

02 · Background

Grammatical metaphor in a constrained medium

Halliday and Martin describe grammatical metaphor as a change in how a meaning is packaged, not merely a change from one word to another. A nominal such as discussion can compress an event into a thing; a Chinese verb such as 討論 restores an action frame. Vinay and Darbelnet's idea of transposition offers a complementary way to describe a shift in word class.

Subtitles add pressure. Lines must remain readable quickly, pronouns are often omitted, and speakers use incomplete clauses. These conditions make verbalisation a useful case for asking how meaning survives when its grammatical shape changes.

02 · 背景

受限媒介中的語法隱喻

Halliday 與 Martin 所說的語法隱喻，改變的是意義的包裝方式，而不只是替換詞語。 discussion 這類名詞把事件壓縮成事物；討論 這類中文動詞則恢復動作框架。 Vinay 與 Darbelnet 的「詞性轉換」概念，則提供了描述詞類變化的另一角度。

字幕還增加了時間與空間壓力。句子必須能快速閱讀，主語常省略， 口語也常含不完整子句。在這些條件下，名詞動詞化正好讓我們追問： 當語法外形改變時，意義如何保留？

03 · Gap

The missing comparison

A corpus list can show hundreds of sentence pairs, but a reader still has to compare them manually. Existing search tools usually stop at keyword lookup. This project links semantic categories, Chinese verb position, bilingual collocations and the underlying table, so a selected term can be followed across different views.

The gap is not that this tiny sample can represent all subtitles. It is that the relationship between the English nominal and the Chinese verbal realisation is hard to see in a flat CSV.

03 · 研究缺口

缺少的比較層次

語料清單能展示數百個句對，讀者卻仍要逐筆比較。既有搜尋工具通常只做到關鍵詞查詢。 本專案把語意類別、中文動詞位置、雙語搭配與原始資料表連動， 讓使用者能沿著同一個詞跨越不同檢視。

研究缺口並非主張這個小樣本能代表所有字幕；真正的問題是， 英文名詞與中文動詞實現之間的關係，很難在一張平面的 CSV 表中看清楚。

04 · Questions

Three linked questions

  1. Which semantic fields dominate the supplied set of nominal-to-verb pairs?
  2. Do those categories differ in whether the Chinese verb begins a line, follows a modal or negative marker, or sits inside a larger predicate?
  3. Which English terms form strong, repeated links with particular Chinese verbs, and what changes when the filters are applied?

04 · 問題

三個互相連動的問題

  1. 在提供的名詞轉動詞資料中，哪些語意領域佔最多？
  2. 不同語意類別，是否在「動詞起首」、「情態或否定語境」與「嵌入謂語」的分佈上有差異？
  3. 哪些英文詞與特定中文動詞形成反覆連線？套用篩選後，圖形如何改變？

05 · Method

From five columns to a linked corpus

Source

500 supplied rows; 500 unique sentence IDs; 195 English key terms; 282 Chinese target verbs.

Alignment

The source and target sentence text, English nominal and Chinese verb are preserved from the supplied CSV.

Coding

Each nominal receives one of four semantic categories using the published codebook and a term-to-category map.

Positions

The target text is searched for the Chinese verb. The marker preceding it determines verb-first, modulated or embedded.

The transformation is reproducible: run python3 scripts/build_corpus.py. Eight repeated sentence pairs remain because they occur at different source IDs; they are documented rather than silently removed.

Provenance limitation. The supplied file identifies sentence IDs but not a download date or sampling seed. The site labels the corpus as OpenSubtitles 2024 en-zh_CN because that matches the course example and sentence-ID format, but this needs confirmation against the original download.

Human check still required. At implementation time, all published rows are marked human_checked: "no". I did not mark 50 rows as checked without the student personally verifying them. The quality-check page prepares a reproducible seeded sample of 50 entries for that task.

Codebook log: version 1 separated speech and thought from action nouns, but pilot coding showed that terms such as question and permission needed separate domains. Version 2 uses the four published categories. The boundary case consideration is coded as law and control because its usage in the supplied lines concerns legal or transactional judgement; it is not treated merely as thinking. Terms that combine two fields are assigned by the core meaning of the nominal in context, not by the Chinese verb alone.

05 · 方法

從五個欄位到連動語料

來源

提供五百行；五百個不同句編號；195 個英文關鍵詞；282 個中文目標動詞。

對齊

原文、譯文、英文名詞與中文動詞均保留自使用者提供的 CSV。

編碼

依公開編碼簿及詞項對類別對照，為每個英文名詞指定四個語意類別之一。

位置

在中文譯文中定位動詞，再依前面的標記判斷「動詞起首」、「情態／否定」或「嵌入謂語」。

整個轉換可重現：執行 python3 scripts/build_corpus.py。 有八組重複句對因來自不同原始句編號而保留，並在網站中明確記錄，沒有靜默刪除。

來源限制。 提供的檔案只有句編號，沒有下載日期與抽樣種子。 網站依課堂範例及句編號格式標示為 OpenSubtitles 2024 en-zh_CN， 但仍須由學生對照原始下載確認。

仍須人工核對。 實作完成時，所有公開資料都標為 human_checked: "no"。在學生本人未核對前，我不能假稱已有五十筆通過。 品質檢查頁已以固定種子準備五十筆樣本，供完成這項工作。

編碼簿紀錄：第一版把言語與思考、行動名詞分開，但二十多筆試編顯示 question 與 permission 需要不同領域。第二版採用目前四個類別。 consideration 位於邊界，依提供語境中的法律或交易判斷歸入「法律與控制」， 不單純視為思考。跨領域詞依名詞在句中的核心意義分類，而不只看中文動詞。

06 · Analysis

What the linked views show

Figure 1. Category distribution

The largest category is Action and change (201 entries), followed by Law and control (114) and Emotion and state (104). Communication is the smallest group (81). This does not mean that English subtitles use more action nouns in general; it describes only the supplied 500-row selection.

Figure 2. Chinese verb-position distribution

Most target verbs are embedded in a larger predicate (281), while 169 appear near modality or negation and 50 are the first meaningful element in the line. The Meaning × verb position treemap lets the reader test whether this overall profile is repeated within each semantic category.

In the Bilingual network map, attention is the clearest hub in the full unfiltered view, largely through its repeated link to 注意. The network controls make that observation checkable rather than purely visual: changing the top-term and minimum-weight thresholds reveals how many links depend on high-frequency repetitions.

The Frequent-term heatmap performs the numeric check that the dramatic network view cannot do alone. Each cell is a count from the same filtered table. Selecting a cell narrows both the English term and semantic category, while the network and treemap redraw from the resulting rows.

Interpretation. Verbalisation makes the subtitle line more predicate-centred, but the data cannot by itself prove why a translator chose it. A likely explanation is compression and readability in dialogue, not a universal rule of English-to-Chinese translation.

06 · 分析

連動圖表揭示了什麼

圖一：語意類別分佈

最大類別是行動與變化（201 筆），其次是 法律與控制（114 筆）與情緒與狀態（104 筆）。 言語與思考最少（81 筆）。這不代表所有英文字幕都偏重行動名詞； 它只描述提供資料中的五百筆選樣。

圖二：中文動詞位置分佈

多數目標動詞嵌在較大的謂語中（281 筆），169 筆鄰近情態或否定標記， 50 筆是句中有意義的第一個成分。意義 × 動詞位置樹狀圖可檢查 每個語意類別是否重複整體趨勢。

在雙語詞語網絡中，未篩選的完整檢視以 attention 為明顯中心，主要透過與 注意 的反覆連線。 網絡控制項讓觀察能被檢驗：調整顯示詞數與最小連結權重，就能看出結果在多大程度上依賴高頻重複。

高頻詞熱圖提供網絡無法單獨完成的數值核對。每一格都直接來自 相同篩選下的資料表；點擊一格會同時收窄英文詞與語意類別， 網絡與樹狀圖也依新的資料列重畫。

解釋。 動詞化使字幕句子更以謂語為中心，但資料本身不能證明譯者為何選擇這種形式。 較合理的解釋是對話的壓縮與可讀性，而不是一條普遍適用的英中翻譯規則。

07 · Conclusion

A small but inspectable corpus explorer

The project turns a flat 500-row CSV into a connected analytical environment. The search and filters expose subsets; the network shows repeated bilingual links; the treemap compares category and verb position; the heatmap offers term-level counts; and the table preserves the original evidence. All views share state and update together.

The main finding is structural: the selected pairs overwhelmingly describe action and change, and most Chinese verbs are embedded rather than line-initial. The more important contribution is the transparent pipeline from source pair to category, position, chart and citation.

07 · 結論

一個小型但可檢驗的語料探索器

本專題把平面化的五百行 CSV 轉為互相連結的分析環境。搜尋與篩選揭露資料子集； 網絡顯示反覆的雙語連結；樹狀圖比較語意與動詞位置；熱圖提供詞項層級計數； 資料表則保留原始證據。所有檢視共用狀態並同步更新。

主要結構發現是：選樣中的句對大量描述行動與變化，多數中文動詞嵌在謂語中， 而非出現在行首。更重要的成果，是從原文句對、類別、位置、圖表到來源之間， 建立可檢查且可重現的流程。

08 · Reflection

What I learned

The hardest part was not drawing a chart. It was deciding what a chart was allowed to claim. A network edge looks authoritative, yet it may represent one sentence pair or a repeated subtitle line. I therefore added minimum-link controls and made the table the final source of truth.

I would improve the project by confirming the original corpus download, recording the true sampling seed, and completing the human check before publication. I would also code a stratified sample separately and compare the two category decisions.

AI was useful for inspecting the brief, writing the data transformation, building the interface and drafting bilingual prose. It could not verify the student's personal judgement, confirm provenance from a missing metadata field, or make subjective coding decisions authoritative.

08 · 反思

我學到什麼

最難的不是畫出圖表，而是決定圖表可以宣稱什麼。網絡連線看起來很有權威， 卻可能只代表一個句對或一組重複字幕。因此我加入最小權重控制， 並讓資料表成為最終核對依據。

若能重做，我會先確認原始語料下載、記錄真正抽樣種子，並在公開前完成人工核對。 我也會另外分層抽樣重新編碼，再比較兩次類別判定。

AI 能協助閱讀作業要求、撰寫資料轉換、建立介面與草擬雙語文字； 但它不能代替學生本人的判斷、無法從缺少的欄位確認來源， 也不應把主觀編碼自動視為最終答案。
